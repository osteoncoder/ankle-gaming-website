Service card image provenance
=============================
The ten WebP images in this folder were generated specifically for this
website using an AI image-generation tool, then resized and compressed for
local use. They are illustrative visuals, not photographs of AnkleGaming's
actual projects or workplaces. They were not downloaded from Unsplash or
Pexels, and no stock-photo attribution or stock license is being claimed.
Several related services intentionally share an image.
