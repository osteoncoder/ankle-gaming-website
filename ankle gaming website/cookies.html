Cookie Policy

AnkleGaming Private Limited · Last updated: November 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. Similar technologies include local storage and server log files.

2. Cookies on This Website

This website is a static site with no advertising or cross-site tracking. As of the date above:

  • We set no first-party cookies for analytics, marketing or tracking. The Site does not run Google Analytics or advertising pixels.
  • Essential processing: our hosting provider may process standard server logs (IP address, browser type, requested pages) for security and reliability. This is not used to build marketing profiles.
  • Third-party fonts: the Site loads fonts from Google Fonts, whose servers may receive your IP address and browser information when delivering font files. See Google's privacy policy for details.
  • External services: if you use our enquiry form, WhatsApp links or email links, those third-party providers (Web3Forms, WhatsApp/Meta, your email provider) apply their own cookie and privacy policies.

3. If This Changes

If we later introduce analytics or marketing cookies, we will update this page and, where required, request your consent before non-essential cookies are set.

4. Managing Cookies

You can block or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings. Blocking cookies will not affect your ability to browse this website.
