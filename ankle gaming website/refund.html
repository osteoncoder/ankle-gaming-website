Refund Policy

AnkleGaming Private Limited · Last updated: November 2026

1. Scope of This Policy

This Refund Policy applies to software development and related services provided by AnkleGaming Private Limited ("AnkleGaming"). Because our work is custom professional services, refunds are assessed against the terms of the signed proposal, statement of work or service agreement ("Project Agreement") for your engagement, which prevails over this general policy if there is any conflict.

2. General Principles

  • Work already performed: fees for milestones, sprints or deliverables that have been completed and/or delivered are generally non-refundable, since senior engineering and design time has been consumed.
  • Unstarted work: amounts paid for work that has not yet begun may be eligible for refund, subject to the Project Agreement.
  • Third-party costs: payments passed through to third parties (domains, hosting, cloud services, app-store fees, licences, stock assets) are non-refundable once incurred.
  • Deposits/advances: booking deposits reserve team capacity and are treated as described in your Project Agreement.

3. Cancellation by the Client

If you choose to cancel an engagement, please notify us in writing at info@anklegaming.com. We will reconcile work completed up to the cancellation date against payments made and confirm any eligible refund amount. Deliverables for unpaid work remain the property of AnkleGaming until paid in full.

4. Cancellation by AnkleGaming

If AnkleGaming is unable to commence or complete an engagement for reasons attributable to us, amounts paid for undelivered work will be refunded in accordance with the Project Agreement.

5. How Refunds Are Processed

Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method (or another mutually agreed method). Processing timelines depend on the payment provider and bank; we will confirm the refund in writing once initiated. Any specific timelines, percentages or milestone rules in your Project Agreement apply.

6. Disputes

If you believe a charge is incorrect, contact us first at info@anklegaming.com or +91 95997 34137 — most issues can be resolved quickly with a review of the project records.
