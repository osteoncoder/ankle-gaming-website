Privacy Policy

AnkleGaming Private Limited · Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AnkleGaming Private Limited ("AnkleGaming", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects personal information that you provide through this website (the "Site"). By using the Site and submitting an enquiry, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Enquiry information: your name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number, the service and enquiry type you select, an optional budget range, and the project details you write in our contact or newsletter forms.
  • Direct communications: information you share when you email, call or message us (including via WhatsApp).
  • Technical information: like most websites, our hosting provider may process standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages visited) for security and reliability.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide information about our services;
  • To prepare proposals, estimates and project communications you request;
  • To send occasional product/engineering updates if you subscribe to our newsletter (you may unsubscribe at any time);

  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Sharing of Information

We may share limited information with service providers who help us operate (for example, our form-processing and email providers) strictly to deliver the service you requested, and where required by law. Our enquiry forms are processed by Web3Forms; please review their privacy policy for their handling of form submissions.

4. Data Retention

Enquiry data is retained only as long as necessary to respond to you, maintain business records, and meet legal requirements. You may request deletion of your personal information at any time (see Section 6).

5. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information. No internet transmission is completely secure, so please avoid sending sensitive confidential documents through the enquiry form.

6. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and may withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time by contacting us.
